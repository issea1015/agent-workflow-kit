Get SEC Form 4 insider trades as JSON or CSV

Every time a CEO, CFO, director or 10% owner of a US-listed company buys or sells shares, they file a Form 4 with the SEC within two business days. The data is public and free. Getting it into a spreadsheet takes a few steps; here they are, plus a shortcut.

1. Map tickers to CIK numbers

EDGAR identifies companies by CIK, not ticker. The SEC publishes the mapping as one JSON file: https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json. Pad the CIK to 10 digits for the next step (NVDA is 0001045810).

2. List the company's recent filings

https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001045810.json returns the company's recent filings as parallel arrays (form, accessionNumber, filingDate, primaryDocument). Keep the rows where form is 4.

Fair-access rules: the SEC asks every automated client to send a User-Agent header that names you and gives a contact email, and to stay under 10 requests per second. Requests without it get blocked.

import json, urllib.request
UA = {"User-Agent": "Your Name your.email@example.com"}
def get(url):
    return json.load(urllib.request.urlopen(urllib.request.Request(url, headers=UA)))
sub = get("https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001045810.json")
r = sub["filings"]["recent"]
form4 = [(r["accessionNumber"][i], r["filingDate"][i]) for i, f in enumerate(r["form"]) if f == "4"]

3. Parse the Form 4 XML

Each filing sits under https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/<CIK>/<accession without dashes>/ and contains an XML document. Each nonDerivativeTransaction element is one trade: transactionDate, transactionCode, transactionShares, transactionPricePerShare, transactionAcquiredDisposedCode (A or D) and sharesOwnedFollowingTransaction. The insider's name and role (isOfficer, officerTitle, isDirector, isTenPercentOwner) are under reportingOwner.

4. Filter to real buys and sells

Most Form 4 rows aren't market trades. The transaction code tells you which are:

  • P: open-market purchase. The one most people look for.
  • S: open-market sale.
  • A: grant or award. M: option exercise. F: shares withheld for tax. G: gift.

Keep P and S, multiply shares by price for a dollar value, and sum per ticker: net buying by several insiders within a few weeks ("cluster buying") is the pattern screeners usually watch.
